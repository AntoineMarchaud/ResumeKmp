About me

Freelance Android developer with 19 years of software engineering, 12 of them on Android.

I built Akinator for Android (100M+ downloads), then worked on demanding consumer apps: Proton Pass (security, millions of users, 99.7% crash-free, rated 4.9 on Google Play), Louis Vuitton’s flagship app (LVMH), Stellantis, Bouygues Telecom (Android TV) and now France Télévisions.
